CHAPTER 10 · 3/5

Why not France?

18th-c. France shone in education, publishing, Enlightenment; John Law’s bank and Mississippi Company converted state debt to equity into a classic bubble — after the bust, capital froze. Costly wars (Augsburg, Spanish Succession, Seven Years’, American Revolution, etc.) outran gains; privileged estates resisted tax; Third Estate erupted — Revolution → Terror → assignat hyperinflation → failed Directorate.¹

Napoleon and the Vienna order

Napoleon, the military hero, centralizes, stabilizes, then overreaches into Russia and loses — British finance and navy are decisive advantages. Congress of Vienna (1814–15) rebuilds balance of power; Britain seeks no vast new European territories, only no new hegemon; “splendid isolation” focuses on trade and colonies — Imperial Century. Enlightenment/reform still push representation and national unifications (Germany, Italy).²

Bubble + war finance + tax privilege = revolution script; Britain wins the hot war with a stronger balance sheet.
