CHAPTER 11 · 1/4

Chapter 11: US rises in the 19th century and gradually surpasses Britain; this chapter digs deeper into dollar reserve status and monetary policy because the story is still live. Eight powers: education once drove innovation and competitiveness; relative edges in education, competitiveness, and trade have fallen, while innovation/tech, reserve status, and financial-center strength remain — but income/balance-sheet and internal conflict look more worrying.¹

Model reading: US roughly 70% ±10% through its Big Cycle — not yet Stage 6 hot civil war, but internal conflict high and rising.²

Same machine, new clothes and currency symbols — read America with a longer monetary lens.
