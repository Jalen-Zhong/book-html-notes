CHAPTER 12 · 6/7

Reform and opening: a one-phrase strategy

Deng’s slogan pairs “reform” (markets and incentives) with “opening up” (learn, trade, interact), framed as socialism/market economy with Chinese characteristics, pursued without threatening stronger foreigners. Full US ties in 1979; roughly 70-year staged goals (food/clothing → quadruple → medium-developed). Collective leadership, term norms, and the 1982 constitution reduce one-man risk and enable Jiang/Hu handovers.¹

Get strong without looking threatening — until the setting changes

From tiny per-capita income, exports, learning, and markets lift wealth and capacity; a US–China symbiosis forms (US buys goods; China holds dollar assets). HK/Macau deals complete part of the humiliation-reversal list. After 2008, advanced-economy gaps and manufacturing resentment rise while China debt-stimulates — the stage shifts toward sharper rivalry.²

Keeping a low profile is not a personality tag — it is rational strategy when power is still asymmetric.
