CHAPTER 12 · 3/7

How history enters the leadership OS

Relative to the US, Chinese leaders more systematically treat history plus Confucian/Taoist/legalist/Marxist thought as decision inputs. Mao intensively read the Comprehensive Mirror and Twenty-Four Histories and wrote On Practice / On Contradiction. Lee Kuan Yew and Deng Xiaoping are the author’s modern samples of “Confucian expectations + capitalist tools” — Deng’s phrase: a socialist market economy with Chinese characteristics.¹

Operating-style highlights

Top-down order, long horizons, high sensitivity to chaos, and posture shifts as relative power changes. Western readers are warned not to use outdated “old communist China” cartoons; triangulate with people who have worked in China. Media distortion and blind loyalty are themselves read as dangerous signs of the times.²

Not “mysterious China” — another order software repeatedly stress-tested.
