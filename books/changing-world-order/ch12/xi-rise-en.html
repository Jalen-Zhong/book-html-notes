CHAPTER 12 · 7/7

From 2012: richer, stronger, more constrained

Xi takes power in 2012 over a richer, more powerful China that is also more indebted (debt growth outruns the economy 2009–12), more corruption-anxious, and increasingly at odds with the US. In 2009 China already told the UN it has “indisputable sovereignty” over large East/South China Sea areas. Policy mix: contain debt while reforming; Made in China 2025 (AI, robotics, semiconductors, pharma, aerospace self-sufficiency/leadership); education/military; Belt and Road; 14th Five-Year Plan targets toward 2035; tighter political control; common prosperity and environment. After Trump’s 2016 election friction goes overt — from “ride-along growth” to “rising challenger.”¹

Power snapshot now

Trade, output, and innovation/tech roughly peer the US; education and military are strong and rising fast; financial-center and reserve-currency status remain clear lags — RMB internationalization and market depth still catch up. Eight-power totals put China second and closing; next chapter splits conflicts into trade, tech, geopolitics, capital, military, culture, and the war with ourselves.²

Reserve currency is the slowest brick — empire ledgers lag factories and labs.
