CHAPTER 5 · 1/4

Chapter 5 maps the big cycle of internal order and disorder: stages like a disease — symptoms locate the stage; the stage shapes remedies and probability ranges. An archetypal full loop is ~100 years (wide variance), with nested ~8-year debt cycles and left–right political swings. Countries sit in different stages; those gaps shape the world order.¹

Six-stage skeleton: (1) new order, leadership consolidates → (2) build/refine resource-allocation and bureaucracy → (3) peace and prosperity → (4) excess spending, debt, widening wealth–political gaps → (5) bad finances and intense conflict → (6) civil war/revolution → back to (1). Peak around (3), trough at (6); rare leaders carry 6→1→2.

Stages are not fate sentences — they are maps of risk and remedy.

Why Stage 5 is the urgent read

Public charts place the US (at writing) in Stage 5 — worsening finances and rising conflict while tech/military strengths remain but decline relatively; China’s relative place differs. After Stage 5 comes either strong peaceful rebuild or slide into Stage 6. Later leaves cover 1–3, 4–5, then 6 markers and leader types.²
