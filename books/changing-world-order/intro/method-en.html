INTRODUCTION · 2/3

On the evening of 15 August 1971, 22-year-old Dalio was summer-clerking on the NYSE floor. Nixon ended the promise to convert paper dollars into gold — a government default, money-as-known stopping. Monday, he expected a crash; instead the dollar fell and stocks jumped about 4%. Shock sent him into the archives: devaluations had hit equities this way before. More painful surprises welded a rule — understand every major economic and market move across 100+ years and major countries.¹

An unseen devaluation is not “impossible”; it is “undersampled.”

Archetypes, algorithms, and if/then

Like a doctor with many cases: qualitative and quantitative work, scholars and practitioners, archives and statistics. Output: an archetypal sequence of rises and declines; deviations explain “why this time differs.” Models become algorithms that monitor reality versus template and mint “if X then Y” principles. With Bridgewater partners he runs this systematically: stay on-template and bet what usually comes next; if off-template, ask why and correct. It is a work in progress — humility earned by being wrong often.²

Perspective shifts: from blizzard fragments to patterns over time — how economic and political cycles interlock. People are ants with crumbs unless they see big cycles and their place in them. Limited personality types, paths, and situations produce repeating stories; costumes, languages, and tech change.

A chain of studies leading here

Long-term debt and capital-market work reframed post-2008: zero rates, QE, widening wealth gaps, populism — mechanically akin to the 1930s. A 2014 cross-country 10-year growth project became productivity/structural-reform research. After 2016 and rising populism, Populism: The Phenomenon aligned 1930s wealth/values fractures. Quintile cuts revealed “two economies” and reform pressure. Decades in China showed a peer competitor across production, trade, tech, geopolitics, and capital markets.³

Three forces he had to understand: (1) the long-term debt and capital markets cycle; (2) the internal order/disorder cycle; (3) the external order/disorder cycle — a true multi-domain rival for the US for the first time in his life.
