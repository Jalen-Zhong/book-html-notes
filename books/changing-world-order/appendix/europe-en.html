APPENDIX · 3/4

Eurozone and Germany

Eurozone (~Aug 2021): expected real growth ~0.3%/decade; non-financial debt ~241%, government debt ~104% of GDP; short rates ~−0.5% with monetization ongoing; top 1%/10% income ~11%/35%; euro ~21% of global reserves and ~22% of global debt denomination. Germany: net IIP ~+71% of GDP; non-financial debt ~183%, government debt ~69%; expected growth also ~0.3% — strong industry/discipline, weak growth, limited reserve role.¹

UK, France, Netherlands, Spain

UK: financial-center and language/network legacies remain strong; post-Brexit trade/investment paths rewire; reserve role far below dollar era. France: high military/diplomatic and EU political weight, with recurring fiscal/reform tugs. Netherlands: open trade and historic finance DNA persist; scale caps empire score. Spain: southern-euro exemplar with wider debt/growth swings. Read Europe as “alliance sum ≠ single sovereign empire.”²

Europe’s power is in the union; Europe’s fragility is in the union’s seams.
