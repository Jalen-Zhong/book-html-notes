APPENDIX · 4/4

Japan and India

Japan: still deep in tech/industry and finance, but demographics, high debt, and slow growth cap empire score; external security increasingly managed via US alliance and China relationship. India: demographics and long-run growth potential lift the trajectory; institutional and infrastructure/education gaps remain; geopolitically a key Indo-Pacific balancer.¹

Russia

Russia: notable military and energy/commodity influence; weak economic diversification plus demographic/institutional gaps depress the composite. The sample already showed “hard power strong, composite constrained” — war after publication pushed that structure onstage. For investors and policy readers: commodities, sanctions, and alliance rewiring are different ticks on one gauge.²

Flank countries rarely decide which peak is No.1 — they set the cost function of peak conflict.
