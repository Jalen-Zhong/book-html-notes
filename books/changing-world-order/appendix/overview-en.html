APPENDIX · 1/4

The appendix prints the author’s computer-generated country-power summaries (sample timing ~Aug 2021 in the book). Readings aggregate hundreds of stats weighted by relevance, quality, and cross-country/time consistency; both quantity and quality matter, tilted toward who would likelier win a contest/war. Gauges share DNA with the final chapter and update at economicprinciples.org.¹

Each country blurb starts with overall empire score (0–1) and rank, then big-cycle readings (econ/financial position, debt burden, expected growth, etc.) and eight-power trajectories. These notes nest as: overview → US & China → Europe majors → Asia & others.²

Treat the appendix as a gauge snapshot, not an eternal medal table.
