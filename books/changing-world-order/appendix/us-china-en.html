APPENDIX · 2/4

United States: still strong, gradually weakening

Sample reading (~Aug 2021): overall power No.1 (empire score ~0.87) on a gradual-decline path. Strengths: equities ~55% of world market cap / ~64% volume; ~55% of global transactions in dollars; patents ~17%, R&D/researchers ~26%, bachelor’s ~20%. Weaknesses: expected real growth ~1.1%/decade; net IIP ~−64% of GDP; non-financial debt ~277%, government debt ~128% of GDP; short rates ~0.1% with monetization ongoing; top 1%/10% income shares ~19%/45%. ~99% of debts in own currency; reserve privilege intact.¹

China: rising challenger

Sample reading: overall power No.2 (~0.75) on an upward path. Strengths: ~22% of world activity at PPP; bachelor’s ~22%; competitiveness/trade/output plus fast-rising innovation, education, military. Weaknesses: reserve/finance-center lag; non-financial debt ~263% of GDP but government debt ~48%; net IIP ~+12%; expected real growth ~4.3%/decade; short rates ~1.9%; top 1%/10% income ~14%/41%; ~96% debts in own currency. Mid-ascent big-cycle position with widening US conflict surfaces.²

No.1’s fragility is internal + late money; No.2’s is finance lag + external friction.
