APPENDIX · 2/4

United States: still strong, gradually weakening

Sample reading: overall power No.1 (empire score ~0.87) on a gradual-decline path. Strengths: capital markets/financial center, innovation/tech, education, military, reserve currency, output. Weaknesses: unfavorable econ/financial position, large internal conflict. Education, trade importance, and relative military are softening; high debts, low expected real growth, deep negative net IIP — yet debts almost entirely in own currency with reserve privilege intact.¹

China: rising challenger

Sample reading: overall power No.2 (~0.75 scale) on an upward path. Strengths: competitiveness, trade, output, fast-rising innovation and education, military. Weaknesses: reserve currency and financial-center status still lag; debt and some structural imbalances need management. Big-cycle position looks more mid-ascent while US conflict surfaces widen — appendix numbers rhyme with Ch.12–13 narrative.²

No.1’s fragility is internal + late money; No.2’s is finance lag + external friction.
