CHAPTER 14 · 1/5

Chapter 14 shares how the author approaches the future: perceive and adapt to what is happening; assign probabilities to paths; protect against unacceptable tails. Who lives by the crystal ball eats ground glass — so use history to imagine ranges and keep fallbacks for being wrong.¹

Method trio: evolution (long-run productivity up), cycles and bumps (debt bubbles, wars, pandemics), quality indicators (where you sit in the cycle). The close spreads the dashboard and covers the next decade plus betting under known/unknowns.²

Bet on the upside — not so hard that one bump knocks you out.
