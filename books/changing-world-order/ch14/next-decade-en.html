CHAPTER 14 · 5/5

Next 10 years: scenarios, not point forecasts

The author refuses fake-precise point forecasts and stresses monitoring: debt-monetization paths, internal-conflict escalation markers, US–China red-line tests, productivity/inventiveness diffusion, and slow reserve-currency migration. The decade is more likely stacked “late-cycle pressure + tech acceleration + great-power rivalry” than a single story.¹

Known, unknown, and bets

Keep position and hedges for known patterns (cycle repeats); keep diversity and liquidity for unknowns; set hard limits on unacceptable losses. The appendix’s country computer readings supplement the gauges; economicprinciples.org updates ongoing. Humility is not weakness — it is an ops manual after eating glass more than once.²

Treat the book as a gauge manual, not a fortune-ticket printout.
