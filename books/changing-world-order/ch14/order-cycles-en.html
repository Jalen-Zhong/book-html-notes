CHAPTER 14 · 4/5

Internal order/disorder cycle

Watch wealth and values gaps, populism, rule-breaking, compromise capacity, and violence. US internal-conflict gauges are high and rising; the author puts ~30% odds of Stage-6 (civil-war-type) dynamics within 10 years — still high enough to hedge. Public-survey magnitudes: ~15–20% in each party saying the country would be better if large numbers of the other “just died”; parental displeasure at cross-party marriage from ~5% in 1960 to roughly a third–half recently. China stresses common prosperity and anti-corruption/control while managing its own gaps.¹

External order and acts of nature

Externally, rising and ruling powers test red lines (Ch.13 wars). Nature — climate, pandemics, other disasters — can suddenly rewrite productivity and politics; COVID already showed fiscal–monetary coordination as an accelerator. Put internal order, external rivalry, and nature into one risk budget so you are not “right on only one variable.”²

When the inside cracks first, outside fights run hotter; disasters yank the cracks wider.
