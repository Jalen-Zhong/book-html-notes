CHAPTER 14 · 3/5

Human inventiveness: strongest long force

Inventiveness is cast as the long engine that usually outruns cycle noise — yet diffusion depends on education, openness, incentives, and social capital. The digital revolution already broke prior “common sense” (e.g. yields can’t go negative). The art is spotting and adapting to shifts without mistaking a fad for a paradigm.¹

Debt/money/capital-market/economic cycle

Short and long debt cycles remain core gauges: debt vs income, rate room, CB monetization, reserve status, wealth gaps, and political polarization. The US is deep in late-cycle monetization; China faces debt management and structural transition. Synchronized multi-country stimulus can inflate global assets and later cleanup risk.²

Invention raises the ceiling; debt decides how long you live under it.
