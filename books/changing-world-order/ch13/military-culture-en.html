CHAPTER 13 · 4/5

Military war

Hot war remains a low-probability, high-impact tail. Watch military balances (conventional/nuclear/space/cyber), alliance credibility, and miscalculation/accident. Taiwan-type scenarios force the US to choose defend-order vs retreat; for China they are high-pressure core-interest and national-narrative zones. The author does not forecast shooting — he asks readers to treat military escalation as a monitorable sequence.¹

Culture war

Culture war is about which governance model, individual-vs-collective balance, freedom-vs-order mix, media narratives, and education content attract or compel. Both sides cast themselves as the moral high ground; amid domestic polarization, culture narratives weaponize easily and shrink compromise space.²

Deterrence needs clarity; cultural confidence needs restraint — the reverse of each is dangerous.
