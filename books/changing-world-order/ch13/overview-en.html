CHAPTER 13 · 1/5

Chapter 13 shifts from pure fact-telling toward judgment and scenarios: both countries are in several “wars” — trade/economic, technology, geopolitics, capital, military, culture, plus each side’s war with itself. These are not isolated scrapes but faces of one larger conflict; macro play is multidimensional chess with Russia, Japan, India, Australia, Europe, and more.¹

Destiny pushed the US into post-success excess and weakening, and China into post-revolution mutually reinforcing ascent. Dollar reserve status let Americans borrow excessively (including from China), locking both into awkward creditor–debtor ties amid war narratives. History’s lesson: no country sustains strengthening forces forever without producing decline-making excesses.²

Five classic wars + culture war + self-tearing — map the board before Ch.14 probabilities.
