CHAPTER 7 · 1/3

Chapter 7: investing in light of the Big Cycle. Markets are driven mainly by four determinants: growth, inflation, risk premiums, and discount rates. Investing exchanges a lump sum today for future cash flows shaped by growth and inflation; paths are written by moves in the four plus fiscal–monetary interaction. Central banks set money/credit conditions; governments set tax/spend — both reshape markets.¹

Most investors extrapolate one lifetime; stretch samples across decades and countries and the pre-1945 tail appears — wealth destroyed or confiscated in most places, capitalists jailed or killed in some. The point is not scare tactics; it is putting tails into principles.²

Volatility is not the full definition of risk; failing to fund needed future buying power is.
