CHAPTER 9 · 1/3

Chapter 9: de facto independence in 1581; roughly 1625–1795 the Dutch eclipse Habsburgs and China as the richest empire. Tiny population/land couldn’t dominate continental armies, but economy, finance, and navy secured a trading-post empire — making the guilder the first global reserve currency. Peak ~1650: Dutch Golden Age.¹

The story starts with classic Spanish/Habsburg decline: overextension, taxes and coin debasement, Price Revolution, decadent leadership, Reformation and the Thirty Years’ War — opening space for the Dutch rise.²

New empires rise when old ones grow weak and decadent — the Dutch script matches Part I.
