CHAPTER 6 · 2/3

Case: path to WWII. After the 1929 Depression, countries fought internally over wealth → turned to more nationalist, autocratic, militarized leadership; the League could not constrain. Germany: reparations burden, Hitler’s refusal to pay, exit from the League, 1934 autocracy; fascist-style rebuild with big fiscal-monetary stimulus, privatizing yet controlling, militarizing employment. Japan: resource scarcity + military expansion in China. US/UK began with appeasement and domestic priority; economic tools first.¹

Economic–geopolitical steps toward hot war

Classic economic-war tactics include tariffs/quotas, FX and capital controls, asset freezes, cutting clearing, embargoes/blockades (detail at economicprinciples.org). 1937 Hitler secret meeting on expansion; rapid conquests in Europe; Japan’s Shanghai/Nanjing occupation with massive civilian deaths. Roosevelt won re-election promising to stay out while already acting economically; 1941 Lend-Lease; US freezes of Japanese assets and embargoes — vacuums left by weak colonial empires in Asia filled by Japan, colliding with US Pacific aims. Dec 7–8 1941 Pearl Harbor and Philippines attacks linked Pacific and European theaters into a decisive decade.²

Unknowns dwarf knowns: rivals fight when powers are comparable — so both sides enter thinking they can win.
