## Fact / footnote research URLs

### Debt–deflation & recession mechanisms
- Irving Fisher (1933), “The Debt-Deflation Theory of Great Depressions” — public economics primers
- Richard Koo, balance-sheet recession public primers (*The Holy Grail of Macroeconomics* discussions)
- Eggertsson & Krugman (2012) public abstracts on debt-constrained demand
- Mian & Sufi, *House of Debt* — public discussions of debt and inequality; Spanish mortgage/bankruptcy post-crisis reporting

### Euro / US lessons
- Public financial-crisis histories of US subprime securitization (MBS/CDO chains, ratings/leverage loops)
- ECB / euro-area public retrospectives on sovereign–bank “doom loop” dynamics

### China leverage
- Public book-excerpt magnitudes: end-2018 total debt ≈ 258% of GDP; decade debt vs GDP growth contrast
- BIS credit statistics — non-financial credit/GDP; elevated corporate leverage framing
- Public retrospectives on the 2008–09 “4 trillion” stimulus, LGFV expansion, and overcapacity (efficiency / external imbalance / upgrading lenses)

### Deleveraging policies
- State Council Document 43 (2014) / revised Budget Law — local debt ceilings; bond financing channel
- MOF public disclosures on 2015–18 local-government swap bonds (~RMB 12trn scale in public summaries)
- 2015 Central Economic Work Conference supply-side package (“three cuts, one reduction, one strengthening”)
- 2018 Central Financial and Economic Affairs Commission — structural deleveraging (local + SOE focus)
- Public primers on hidden-debt governance (definition unification; curb new implicit commitments)
