## Fact / footnote research URLs

### House prices & household debt
- NBS Statistical Communiqué on 2019 National Economic and Social Development — permanent urbanization ~60.6% vs hukou urbanization ~44.38%
- IMF Working Paper WP/19/258 *Assessing Macro-Financial Risks of Household Debt in China* — household debt ~52.6% of GDP (end-2018); housing-related debt ~2/3 of household debt
- PBOC Statistics Department 2019 urban household balance-sheet survey — housing ~70% of assets; mortgages ~75.9% of liabilities; debt participation ~56.5%
- Reuters / public summaries of the PBOC survey; trickle-down consumption public economics discussions
- Public primers on mortgage down-payment buffers vs U.S. subprime packaging (chapter-structure aligned)

### Hukou / factor-market reform
- Hukou and migration academic reviews (e.g. Afridi et al. on social identity; China Economic Review / World Development hukou-reform studies)
- LSE/CFM discussion paper on mobility barriers, land rental frictions, and employment allocation
- NBER w29666 *Land Security and Mobility Frictions* — complementarity of land security and labor mobility
- Public policy narratives on hukou easing, three-rights separation, and people-centered urbanization; 19th Party Congress principal-contradiction reframing (public)

### Inequality
- Hirschman “tunnel effect” public economics primers
- Public development discussions of China’s mass poverty reduction vs domestic inequality
- Reading notes / TOC-aligned glosses cautioning against a simple Kuznets inverted-U automatic convergence
