      <h2 id="s5">Contagion: money-market pullback and multi-strategy deleveraging</h2>
      <p>The transmission was not mysterious: money markets and funding desks pulled risk; multi-strategy hedge funds, facing redemptions and margin, sold whatever still had a bid — relative value, convertibles, and equity long/short got dragged together.</p>
      <p>Goldman’s Global Alpha and similar quant/multi-strategy books became public exhibits that “smart money” can gap lower when everyone deleverages at once. Returns once credited as alpha partly reflected liquidity beta — a risk that looks like skill in crowded trades and like a trap in a scramble.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn6"><sup class="fn">⁶</sup></a> Carhart-style factor models can speak to momentum, value, and size, yet often omit the dimension where financing liquidity suddenly gets expensive.</p>
