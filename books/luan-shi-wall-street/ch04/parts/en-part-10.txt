          Series 7 (General Securities Representative) is a core FINRA qualification covering corporate securities, munis, options, governments, and more. Series 63 (Uniform Securities Agent State Law) focuses on state “blue sky” agent registration. Exams gate legal practice; they do not certify a stable P&amp;L — his ~95% joke lands exactly there: standardized ticket, then the real class.
