## Fact / footnote research URLs

### Achilles’ heel metaphor
- Classical myth tradition (Thetis / Styx / heel) and English idiom usage for a fatal hidden weakness
- Crisis literature framing wholesale short-term funding dependence as the system’s soft spot beneath Greek-letter risk models

### Money-market funds
- SEC / Investment Company Institute explainers on money-market fund objectives (principal stability, liquidity, short high-quality paper)
- Crisis histories of 2007–08 short-end stress and ABCP exposure in cash-like vehicles; later Reserve Primary Fund “break the buck” (Sep 2008) as related mechanism (slightly after chapter core window)

### Repo haircuts
- Federal Reserve Bank of New York explainers on repurchase agreements and haircuts (collateral cushion / leverage dial)
- Academic and crisis narratives of 2007–08 haircut differentiation: Treasuries vs MBS / structured collateral; refusal of weaker names

### ABCP / SIV / TOB
- Standard 2007–08 histories of asset-backed commercial paper conduit runs and structured investment vehicle rollover failure
- Municipal-finance materials on tender option bond (TOB) structures (long munis → floating certificates + residual; liquidity/bank support dependence)
- Sponsor-bank onboarding when pipelines could not roll (Citi / Merrill as narrative anchors)

### Kerviel / Société Générale (Jan 2008)
- Société Générale newsroom “Kerviel case” timeline: discovery ~18–20 Jan 2008; ~€50B notional European index futures; unwind; 24 Jan announcement of ~€4.9B net loss
- General Inspection report summaries (May 2008) on position build-up and fictitious hedges
- Contemporaneous Risk.net / press coverage of liquidation overlapping global equity volatility

### Fed January 2008 emergency cut
- FOMC statement 22 Jan 2008: **75** bp intermeeting cut of federal funds target to 3.5%; discount rate moved in parallel
- FOMC statement 30 Jan 2008: additional **50** bp cut to 3%
- FRB Monetary Policy Report narrative of Jan 21 conference call and market strains
- Note: book narrative may compress “emergency ~50 bp”; footnotes separate the two public actions

### ARS / Dutch auction freeze (Feb 2008)
- CRS report on auction-rate securities; Chicago Fed Letter on ARS market decline
- FRBSF and academic papers on municipal ARS auction failures after dealer backstops withdrew mid-February 2008
- Wikipedia / SEC and state AG settlement coverage of subsequent par buybacks for retail/institutional holders
