      <p>The Greek hero Achilles was nearly invulnerable—except at the heel his mother held when she dipped him in the Styx.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn1"><sup class="fn">¹</sup></a> The author aims that metaphor at Wall Street: Greek-letter risk models glitter on every screen—VaR, correlations, stress tests—yet the lethal addiction is to <strong>short-term funding</strong>. When overnight and ultra-short cash vanishes, the finest model is only embroidery on armor.</p>
      <p>Chapter 10 pushed core-bank writedowns onto the stage; this chapter digs one layer deeper: writedowns wound capital, but the heel wound is whether you can <strong>roll funding every morning</strong>.</p>
