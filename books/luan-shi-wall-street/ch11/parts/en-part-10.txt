          <strong>⁵ Kerviel / Société Générale (January 2008)</strong>
          In mid-to-late January 2008, Société Générale uncovered trader Jérôme Kerviel’s concealed directional European equity-index futures positions totaling roughly €50 billion notional (hidden via fictitious hedges). The bank unwound around January 21–23 and announced on January 24 a net loss of about €4.9 billion. The unwind overlapped a violent global equity move, making it hard to separate “macro panic” from “one bank’s forced liquidation”—the chapter’s “Fed fooled by appearances” points at that confusion.
