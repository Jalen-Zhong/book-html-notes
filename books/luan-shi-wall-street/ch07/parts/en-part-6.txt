      <p>Product design echoed hedge-fund leverage in retail clothing: teaser rates made the first payments look affordable; cash-out refinances turned price appreciation into spendable cash. For borrowers it was leveraged consumption and speculation; for Wall Street it was feedstock for the securitization machine.</p>
