      <p>Money did not vanish; it changed lanes—energy, emerging markets, and bolder leveraged buyouts<a class="fn-link" href="#fn7"><sup class="fn">⁷</sup></a>. Blackstone’s Equity Office takeout, KKR’s TXU-scale ambitions, bridge loans and “Triple Play”–style package financing let deals sprint before permanent capital arrived. China’s forming sovereign investor (later CIC) took roughly $3 billion of non-voting Blackstone IPO-related units—global spare cash with a proper noun<a class="fn-link" href="#fn8"><sup class="fn">⁸</sup></a>.</p>
      <p>Irony shared the season: Sam Zell sold Equity Office near the CRE peak to Blackstone, then levered into Tribune—selling peak bricks, buying newspaper futures. In boom time, stories outran risk models.</p>
