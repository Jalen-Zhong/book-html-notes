      <p>The author reaches for a dose metaphor: securitization is medicine—useful in moderation to move credit risk to those who can bear it; toxic in excess when the same risk is repackaged again and again. CDOs-squared took already-sliced mezzanine and sliced it once more, farther from the original borrower, while models still printed “acceptable” default correlations<a class="fn-link" href="#fn4"><sup class="fn">⁴</sup></a>.</p>
      <p>The chain was institutional as well as product: banks, raters, insurers, money funds, and cross-border buyers on the same deck. Global finance became mutually collateralized planking—admired as engineering until the screws proved shared.</p>
