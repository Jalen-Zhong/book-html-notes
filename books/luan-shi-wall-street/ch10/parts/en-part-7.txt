      <p>Merrill, around September 2006, bought First Franklin—folding subprime origination into its securitization machine for vertical integration: source loan → package → distribute. In a bull market that is efficiency; in a crash it welds toxin into the balance sheet.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn2"><sup class="fn">²</sup></a></p>
      <p>“Toxic waste” entered everyday speech: not a lab term, but traders’ curse for structured sludge that would not sell, would not mark cleanly, and still sat on books.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn6"><sup class="fn">⁶</sup></a> Washington Mutual, UBS, RBS and others joined the same casualty list. Merrill-linked CDS was chanted near ~500 bps—insurance cost itself became a fear gauge.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn3"><sup class="fn">³</sup></a></p>
