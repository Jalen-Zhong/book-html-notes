      <h2 id="s1">Late-October double top: buyers hibernate</h2>
      <p>In late October 2007, the chill of the equity double top seeped onto the desk. Screens still looked “healthy,” but the texture of two-way markets had changed: bid lists grew longer while real buyers hibernated—calls went out and came back as watchful silence.</p>
      <p>The “lucky” Tennessee housing bond flipped into regret: marks moved the wrong way, and a stop-loss was nothing like cutting Multso rice. Housing credit was thin; counterparties scarce; selling into that void could itself become a second shock.</p>
