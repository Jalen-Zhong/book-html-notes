      <p>Colleague Hu You found a bid around 4.70%—about 20 basis points wider than the earlier ~4.50% anchor. A small number, but a thermometer: the Street was re-sticker-pricing the same paper with a harsher yield.</p>

      <h2 id="s2">Citi and Merrill: megabank writedowns</h2>
      <p>What tore open “the crisis seems past” were core-bank writedowns: Citigroup on the order of ~$10 billion and Merrill Lynch ~$9.4 billion in asset hits and loss expectations, dragging the fight from the fringe back into Wall Street’s chest.<a class="fn-link" href="#fn1"><sup class="fn">¹</sup></a></p>
      <p>The transmission path was no longer fuzzy: edge originators and mortgage pipes first, then hedge funds forced to de-lever and dump, then core commercial and investment banks’ warehouses and CDO inventory. Indexes could still pretend calm; P&amp;L and valuation footnotes were already bleeding.</p>
