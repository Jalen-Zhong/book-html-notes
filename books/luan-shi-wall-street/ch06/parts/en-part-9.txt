      <h2 id="s6">History rhymes: the never-fading amaranth</h2>
      <p>The flower’s name is Amaranth — a plant genus whose etymology carries “unfading.” In 2006, hedge fund Amaranth Advisors managed on the order of $9 billion and became a living irony<a class="fn-link" href="#fn5"><sup class="fn">⁵</sup></a>: a name that promised never to wilt, a book that withered in weeks.</p>
      <p>For the author this is not distant gossip but live evidence that chaos is returning: gilded calm can be torn open by one flower’s fall.</p>
