      <p>Early returns look surgically bright: volatility seems tamed, spread trades tick like a precision clock. Outsiders treat the firm as proof that markets can be domesticated by equations.</p>

      <h2 id="s3">Spreads, leverage, and the “genius” brand</h2>
      <p>LTCM’s core script is not mystical: bet that spreads between related bonds or instruments will converge, then amplify thin edges with financing and derivatives<a class="fn-link" href="#fn2"><sup class="fn">²</sup></a><a class="fn-link" href="#fn3"><sup class="fn">³</sup></a>. Success eats itself — copycats crowd in, spreads compress; to keep the same profit, leverage must rise. Public post-mortems often cite balance-sheet leverage around ~25×, with derivatives notionals pushing “invisible leverage” still higher.</p>
      <p>The more genius in the room, the less room left; the more confident the model, the thinner the margin for error.</p>
