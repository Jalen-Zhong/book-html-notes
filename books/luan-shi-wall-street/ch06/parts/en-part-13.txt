      <h2 id="s11">Canary: the gilded age is ending</h2>
      <p>In the author’s telling, Amaranth is not merely an unlucky fund — it is a canary in the mine: a flower can die first in the gas market while larger cracks in credit and funding pipes still wait. 2006’s calm is not a safety certificate; it is the most deceptive scenery near the end of a gilded age.</p>
      <p>Cyclical history stops sounding like nostalgia and starts sounding like a warning: linear optimism still says “never fading,” while petals are already on the floor.</p>
