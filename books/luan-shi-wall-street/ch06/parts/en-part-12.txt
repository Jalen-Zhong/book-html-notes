      <p>Worse is the lag: after hikes, risk appetite can stay euphoric for a while; liquidity does not <em>feel</em> cold overnight. Policy tightens while markets still add risk — until one lonely leveraged bet blows up and everyone admits the tide already left.</p>

          <strong>⁸ Shadow banking / repo and haircuts</strong>
          Repo finances positions with securities as collateral; a haircut is the discount between collateral value and cash lent. Lower haircuts mean higher leverage; rising haircuts force de-leveraging. Work by Gorton, Metrick, and others emphasizes that crisis “runs” often hit repo and wholesale funding that behaves like runnable deposits. A hedge fund’s invisible leverage frequently lives in these daily-rolled contracts.
