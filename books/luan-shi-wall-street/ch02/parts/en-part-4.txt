vel I tools and terms, Level II valuation application, Level III portfolio management and integration (with newer pathway options at III). After exams and work experience, candidates may become charterholders. For rates and bond seats, the CFA often signals “I seriously studied investments” — the author had already finished Level III at Berkeley, so Rick’s bond talk synced immediately.
          <div class="why">Why it matters: explains how “CFA + bonds” builds instant common language at a fair — and echoes Chapter 1’s signaling story.</div>
          <div class="src">Sources: CFA Institute public program overviews</div>
        </div>
        <div class="fn-item" id="fn10">
          <strong>¹⁰ Bank proprietary trading circa 2005</strong>
          Before the crisis, large banks commonly ran prop desks that traded the firm’s own capital, with successful traders taking a slice of P&amp;L. The 2005 hiring market still sold prop as a playground for the sharp. Post-2008 regulation, especially the U.S. Volcker Rule under Dodd-Frank, sharply limited deposit-taking banks’ proprietary trading; many desks moved to hedge funds or shut. Read this chapter knowing “Lucy Bank” prop is a pre-Volcker species.
          <div class="why">Why it matters: without that era marker, you miss why prop felt like the casino skill set promoted into a real job.</div>
          <div class="src">Sources: eFinancialCareers 2004–05 prop pay/culture pieces; Volcker Rule background primers</div>
