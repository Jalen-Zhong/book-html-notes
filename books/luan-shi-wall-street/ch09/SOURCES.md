### Discount rate vs funds rate (17 Aug 2007)
- Federal Reserve Board press release *Federal Reserve Board discount rate action* (17 Aug 2007): primary credit cut 50bp to 5¾%; spread to funds target narrowed to 50bp; term financing up to 30 days renewable
- Follow-on approvals for other Reserve Banks same day; first funds-rate cut 18 Sep 2007
