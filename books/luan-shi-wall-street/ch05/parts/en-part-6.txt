      <p>The answer is not “three geniuses.” It is the investment bank’s revenue mix: traditional IB (M&amp;A advisory, ECM/DCM underwriting), market-making and sales &amp; trading, plus shadow-banking securitization pipelines, plus a growing pile of principal / proprietary investing<a class="fn-link" href="#fn1"><sup class="fn">¹</sup></a><a class="fn-link" href="#fn2"><sup class="fn">²</sup></a>.</p>
      <p>The muni desk is one cut of that pie. It drinks cheap liabilities, inventory spreads, and sometimes the turnover speed tangled up with structuring and repo finance. Three heads look small; behind them sit the firm’s balance sheet, sales franchise, and funding pipes.</p>
