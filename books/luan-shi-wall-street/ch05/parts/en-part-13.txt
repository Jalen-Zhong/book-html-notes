      <h2 id="s10">A ~$1B muni book on overnight repo</h2>
      <p>Cut back to Lucy: in the telling, the muni book runs on the order of a billion dollars, mostly financed by rolling overnight repo; dealers haircut the collateral, cash arrives, and tomorrow you roll again<a class="fn-link" href="#fn10"><sup class="fn">¹⁰</sup></a>. In calm weather, carry and turnover feel like free lunch. If the repo market refuses to roll — haircuts jump, eligible collateral narrows — a billion of inventory becomes a stone with nowhere to fund.</p>
      <p>This is the chapter’s clearest crisis foreshadow: not a date for house prices, but a map of where the ROE machine’s power cord plugs in — a socket that must be reinserted every night.</p>
