      <h2 id="s11">Short-term structures, hedges, blackjack echo</h2>
      <p>Desks also run SIV- and muni TOB-style short-term structures: short liabilities against longer or more complex assets, harvesting carry and turnover. Rate risk is often swapped away — hedges damp volatility, so models and managers conclude they can “safely” add leverage<a class="fn-link" href="#fn11"><sup class="fn">¹¹</sup></a>.</p>
      <p>The author echoes Chapter 1’s blackjack / standard-deviation intuition: you think you cut volatility; you may only have resized the chips inside the same game. A hedge reshapes risk; it does not make DuPont’s third factor free.</p>
