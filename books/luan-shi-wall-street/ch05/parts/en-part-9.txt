      <h2 id="s6">Walmart mode: ABS/CDO warehouse turnover</h2>
      <p>When margin is not enough, chase turnover. Pig points to “Walmart mode”: thin margin, furious volume — assets should not linger. Pre-crisis, that often meant ABS/CDO warehouse pipelines: aggregate loans or bonds, tranche them, sell them; keep unsold stubs on book for traders to “manage” and work out<a class="fn-link" href="#fn6"><sup class="fn">⁶</sup></a>.</p>
      <p>Fast turnover can flattering ROE even with mediocre margins — if the downstream bid and short-term funding stay open. When the pipe clogs, turnover becomes inventory.</p>
