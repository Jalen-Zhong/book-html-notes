§9 is the modern deflationary mega-case Dalio lived through as trader and policy interlocutor. Template read: housing and shadow-bank leverage → 2007 top cracks → 2008 depression and Lehman systemic crash → 2009 coordinated stimulus and stress tests toward beautiful deleveraging → 2010–11 transition between “sweet spot” and more ease. Particulars (securitization, derivatives, wholesale funding) sit on the same debt-cycle generalities.¹

Versus §8: faster cuts, larger guarantees and QE, stress tests rebuild capital—ugliness shortened. Versus §7: dollar reserve status biases deflation, not hyperinflation. Alternate investor and policymaker hats while reading.²

If “innovation” only moves leverage outside the regulatory umbrella, the rain starts outside the umbrella.

Subs: bubble → top → crash/depression → beautiful deleveraging → aftermath. The book’s BDO excerpts and newsfeed become causal story here—no proprietary positions.³
