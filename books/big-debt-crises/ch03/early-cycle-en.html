1. Early Cycle: Goldilocks

Big Debt Crises §3 Deflationary cycle · sub-chapter · Ray Dalio · story rewrite

Early on, debt may grow quickly yet not faster than incomes — borrowing funds income-raising activity. Burdens are low; balance sheets healthy; room to lever up. Debt, growth, and inflation are neither too hot nor too cold: Goldilocks.¹

Tension is light: danger is how success rewrites risk appetite — healthy leverage is the bubble’s prep school.²

Goldilocks raises no alarm; she only makes people think the storm was cancelled.

Policy moral: targeted macroprudential brakes now beat violent tightening later — few want to brake on a sunny day.³

Section notes
¹ Goldilocks A window of moderate growth/inflation with still-sustainable leverage.
Why it matters：Marks healthy leverage.
Sources：Macro news history; Dalio stages
² Productive credit Loans that raise capacity and income stabilize debt/income — §1 subway logic.
Why it matters：Early positive benchmark.
Sources：§1
³ Macroprudential timing Tighten where bubbles germinate rather than slam the whole economy later.
