§3 is the staged play of a classic deflationary long-term debt cycle. Averaging many cases, Dalio sketches seven phases: early → bubble → top → depression → beautiful deleveraging → pushing on a string → normalization.¹

Typical soil: unsustainable debts mostly in local currency. Busts bring forced selling and defaults more than inevitable FX collapse. Near the zero bound, the four levers — especially printing and guarantees — turn ugly deflation into a beautiful deleveraging.²

Treat the seven sub-chapters as one serial: one debt curve, seven weathers.

Read in order; for remedies first, jump to Depression and Beautiful deleveraging.³
