# Community guidelines

Welcome. This repo hosts **story-first, research-enriched HTML reading notes** for books — Chinese + English — published via GitHub Pages so more people can learn in the open.

## What we are
- Open notes and study guides, **not** pirated ebooks.
- Parallel **zh / en** HTML pages with footnotes, diagrams, and sourced background.
- A place to contribute new books following the same skills and layout.

## What we are not
- A mirror for full copyrighted books or scanned PDFs.
- A place for hate, harassment, or doxxing.
- A guarantee of academic or legal advice.

## Ground rules
1. **Copyright first.** Only use public trial reads, publisher blurbs, interviews, encyclopedia history, or text you own/have rights to. Retell in original wording. Never paste long verbatim protected text.
2. **Mark clearly.** Every page must say it is a rewrite/guide; footnotes are off-book research; readers should buy the official edition for the full work.
3. **Be accurate.** Do not invent quotes, dates, or sources. If you lack a public narrative for a chapter, ship a **theme + public-history research guide** and label it as such.
4. **Be kind.** Review PRs on substance; no personal attacks.
5. **One book per PR** when possible; keep diffs reviewable.

## How to join
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md). Skills live under [`skills/`](./skills/).

## Code of conduct (short)
- Respect contributors and readers.
- No spam, malware, or credential harvesting in pages or scripts.
- Maintainers may reject or revert contributions that break copyright or safety rules.
